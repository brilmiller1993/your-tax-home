Service bureau operator curriculum

BUREAU ADMIN ONLY

1 · Office setup and EFIN controls. Verify each downstream office’s legal name, EFIN approval, authorized responsible official, secure document handling, software setup, and e-file readiness. An EFIN is assigned to an approved e-file provider; never borrow another office’s number.

2 · Bank product enrollment and client disclosures. Train staff to complete the current provider and software enrollment, follow the provider’s identity and account verification steps, use clear fee and loan disclosures, collect required taxpayer consent, and explain that bank products are optional and subject to eligibility and terms. Refund transfers and advances are financial products; do not promise approval, timing, or a specific refund.

3 · Monitoring and escalation. Review office access, rejected submissions, complaints, suspected fraud, fee disputes, and training completion. Preserve records under the provider agreement and the firm’s security plan; route bank underwriting or product questions to the bank provider.

Provider requirements change and differ by tax software, office structure, state, and product. Use the current enrollment checklist and compliance training from the provider for each office.
